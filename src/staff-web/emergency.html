Emergency access

The shop-wide password, for two situations only: setting up the very first Owner account, or getting back in after every Owner account has been lost. It grants every section and puts nobody's name on anything.

Back to normal sign in

Once an Owner account exists this door closes on its own, and any session opened through it stops working. To reopen it after losing every Owner, set STAFF_EMERGENCY_LOGIN=true, sign in here, make a new Owner, then unset it again.
